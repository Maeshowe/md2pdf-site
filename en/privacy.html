Privacy

md2pdf collects no data, and the app itself sends nothing anywhere. Everything happens on your device.

Documents

The app reads the text you open or paste only to create the PDF, on your device. It accesses the clipboard only when you paste: with the Paste button or into a text field. When you send a file from another app, iOS hands over a copy; the app deletes it once it has been read. If the app stops before reading it, the copy is deleted the next time the app stays open for about a minute.

PDF

The finished PDF is kept in the app’s temporary folder so that you can share or save it. It is deleted when you leave the result screen or close its window; if the app stops first, it is deleted at the next launch. Where it goes from there (sharing, saving, printing) is up to you.

Options

The options you choose (table of contents, landscape pages, cards, language) are stored on your device. The title, subtitle and date are not saved: they apply only to the document currently open.

No network

The app contains no networking code, analytics, advertising, accounts or third-party SDKs. It works the same without an internet connection. The links to this notice and the support page are opened by the system in your browser, not by the app.

Analytics

If you have allowed your device to share analytics with app developers in Settings, Apple may share crash reports and statistics about how you use the app with the developer. This is handled by iOS, not by the app.

TestFlight

If you try the beta through TestFlight, Apple passes on to the developer crash reports, the feedback you send including screenshots and, if you were invited by email, your name and address. We use these only to fix bugs and delete them on request. Please use only invented documents in screenshots and feedback.

Support emails

If you email the developer, we use your message and address only to reply. We keep them for at most 6 months after the matter is closed and delete them within 30 days on request. Please do not send real documents by email.

Children

md2pdf is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this notice changes, we update its date and mention the change in the release notes.
