How do I open a document?

With “Open File”, the Paste button, or from another app’s share sheet. On the empty start screen, “Open Sample” loads an invented document so you can try “Create PDF” right away.

Accented letters look wrong. What can I do?

The app expects UTF-8; it recognises older Windows-1250 files and tells you so. If accents still look wrong, save the file with UTF-8 encoding.

The app says there is not enough memory.

Follow the app’s message. If free memory is low, close other apps. If the document is too complex, split it into smaller parts; large tables need the most memory. If the app names the table of contents as the cause, turn it off.

What does this version not do?

Images and formulas are not typeset: an image is replaced by its description, a formula by its source, with a warning. Characters the built-in fonts do not include (for example Chinese, Japanese and Korean characters and many emoji) appear as empty rectangles, with a warning. PDFs are always A4. There is no editor and no Mac version. On iPad, ⌘N does not open a new window; open one from the app icon.

Which systems and languages does it support?

iOS and iPadOS 26 or later. The interface is in Hungarian and English; the document language (hyphenation, table of contents title) can also be Hungarian or English.

Where are the policy and contact details in the app?

Under the ⓘ button on the start screen: Privacy and Support.
