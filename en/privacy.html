Privacy

md2pdf collects no data, and the app itself sends nothing anywhere. Everything happens on your device.

Documents

The app reads the text you open or paste only to create the PDF, on your device. It accesses the clipboard only when you paste: with the Paste button or into a text field. When you send a file from another app, iOS hands over a copy; the app deletes it once it has been read. If the app stops before reading it, the copy is deleted the next time the app stays open for about a minute.

PDF

The finished PDF is kept in the app’s temporary folder so that you can share or save it. It is deleted when you leave the result screen or close its window. If the app stops first, or you close the window while the print sheet is open, it is deleted at the next launch, along with any print copy the system made. Where it goes from there (sharing, saving, printing) is up to you.

Options

The options you choose (table of contents, landscape pages, cards, language) are stored on your device. The title, subtitle and date are not saved: they apply only to the document currently open.

No network

The app contains no networking code, analytics, advertising, accounts or third-party SDKs. Creating a PDF needs no internet connection; a file not yet downloaded from iCloud is fetched by iOS, which does need a connection. The links to this notice and the support page are opened by the system in your browser, not by the app.

Analytics

If you have allowed your device to share analytics with app developers in Settings, Apple may share crash reports and statistics about how you use the app with the developer. This is handled by iOS, not by the app. According to Apple’s notice, this data is aggregated or in a form that does not personally identify you. We use it only to improve the app. To turn it off, go to Settings, Privacy & Security, Analytics & Improvements, and turn off Share With App Developers.

Support emails

If you email the developer, we use your message and address only to reply and to fix the problem you report. We keep them for at most 6 months after the matter is closed and delete them within 30 days on request. Please do not send real documents by email.

Personal data

The app itself collects no data. This section and the ones after it, through Right to object, cover the personal data that reaches the developer: when you email the developer, when the developer invites you as a tester, or when Apple passes it on through TestFlight. The data controller is the developer (see Contact). The legal basis is GDPR Article 6(1)(f), legitimate interests: to reply to people who write, to fix the problems they report and to try the app with testers. Our written balancing test found that your interests and rights do not override these interests; you can get its outcome on request. Writing and testing are voluntary, but without an address we cannot reply or send an email invitation. We make no automated decisions.

TestFlight data: source and retention

The source of TestFlight data is Apple, except your name and email address if you were invited by email: the developer entered those for the invitation, having received them from you or from the person who suggested you. According to Apple’s notice, besides the items listed under TestFlight we also receive other technical data, such as your device model and a device identifier, the system version, the language and, with feedback, the time zone and free disk space. We see TestFlight data in App Store Connect and Xcode and do not keep it beyond the time Apple holds it there, except for device models and system versions, without names, recorded in our internal release records. According to Apple’s notice, it keeps feedback for one year and may keep crash logs and usage data until bugs are resolved. We keep your name and email address for as long as you are a tester. What Analytics says applies to the app installed from the App Store. According to Apple’s notice, the collection of crash and usage data in TestFlight cannot be turned off, and this data may include your name and email address.

Recipients and transfers

The support address is run by Apple’s iCloud Mail service, so Apple stores your emails; TestFlight data is also held in Apple’s systems. We do not pass your data to anyone other than Apple, unless the law requires it. Your data may reach countries outside the European Economic Area (EEA), mainly the United States. About its own handling of data, Apple says that the controller of the personal data of people in the EEA is Apple Distribution International Limited (Ireland). Apple also says that personal data may be accessed by entities around the world, including Apple-affiliated companies, that it is generally stored by Apple Inc. in the United States, and that transfers from the EEA are governed by Standard Contractual Clauses (GDPR Article 46). You can ask Apple for a copy: apple.com/legal/privacy/contact.

Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, for it to be corrected or deleted, or for its processing to be restricted; Articles 15 to 18 of the GDPR set the conditions. The right to data portability does not apply to processing based on legitimate interests. Send your request to the address below. It is free, and we will answer within one month at the latest. You can also manage your TestFlight data held by Apple at privacy.apple.com. If we cannot delete a crash report ourselves, we will tell you so in our reply. You can complain to the Hungarian National Authority for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (NAIH, 1055 Budapest, Falk Miksa utca 9-11; postal address: 1363 Budapest, Pf. 9; naih.hu), or to the supervisory authority of the EU member state where you live or work or where the alleged infringement took place. You can also go to court, including in the member state where you live; in Hungary you can also sue before the regional court for your home or place of residence.

Right to object

You can object at any time, on grounds relating to your particular situation, to the processing of your data based on legitimate interests: write to the address below. We will then stop processing it, unless we can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests, rights and freedoms, or we need it to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

Children

md2pdf is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

On request we delete your feedback and remove you from the testers; crash reports are managed by Apple. Please use only invented documents in screenshots and feedback.
